FlightSmith

Evaluate OpenVSP aircraft geometries with aerodynamic, stability, and control analysis.

FlightSmith is a web application by Marco Mangiola. It converts OpenVSP .vsp3 geometry to AVL format and runs analyses using OptVL, with user-selected aircraft inputs and flight conditions.

Open FlightSmith

The application is currently in private testing. Access is limited to authorized viewers.

From geometry to results

  • Upload an OpenVSP model or load a bundled example aircraft.
  • Review geometry previews and control assignments, and download the converted AVL file.
  • Run aerodynamic coefficients, stability and control derivatives, and trimmed flight-condition solutions.
  • Explore alpha and beta sweeps, plots, and downloadable results.

FlightSmith Support

FlightSmith Support handles problem reports, questions, and feature suggestions. The support form is being configured. For now, contact marcomangiola4614@gmail.com.

When enabled, the form saves your report and optional screenshots or OpenVSP files privately, assigns a ticket number, and queues an email confirmation. The owner receives the report and download links to its attachments.

The owner authorizes Gmail sending access for these support emails. Visitors do not connect their Gmail accounts to the support form.
